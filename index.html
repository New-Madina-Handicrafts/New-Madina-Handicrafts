Care Guide
🐑 Wool Shawls Care
Hand wash gently in cold water only
Use a mild, wool-safe detergent — never harsh chemicals
Do not bleach or use oxidising agents on any colour
Do not tumble dry — this will cause irreversible shrinkage
Dry flat in the shade, away from direct sunlight
Steam iron on low heat if needed — avoid direct iron contact
Store folded in a breathable cotton bag to preserve shape
🧵 Other Fabric Shawls
Machine wash on a gentle, cool cycle is permitted
Use normal mild detergent suitable for delicates
Avoid bleach on coloured fabrics to preserve vibrancy
Iron at medium heat setting with a pressing cloth
Hang to dry in shade or lay flat on a clean surface
✨ Styling Tips
Drape over one shoulder for a classic Swati look
Wrap fully around the shoulders for warmth on cool evenings
Use as a table runner or wall hanging to display the artistry

Our Story

A Legacy Woven in Every Thread

Founded in 1950 in the picturesque valley of Islampur, Swat, New Madina Handicrafts has been a guardian of the ancient art of shawl-making for over seven decades. What began as a small family workshop has grown into a symbol of premium craftsmanship.

Each piece tells a story — of skilled artisans who have inherited their craft through generations, of mountain wool carefully selected for its softness, and of patterns that echo the breathtaking beauty of the Swat Valley. Every shawl begins from raw material — the finest Raah Maal — and passes through multiple careful stages before it reaches your hands.

The Process

The Art of Making

From mountain pastures to your shoulders — every step crafted with devotion

🐑
Natural Wool
We source the finest Pashmina and Merino wool from the high-altitude pastures of Swat, ensuring unmatched softness and warmth.
🧵
Hand Spinning
Skilled artisans hand-spin each yarn using traditional spinning wheels passed down through generations of master craftsmen.
🎨
Natural Dyeing
We use plant-based natural dyes to create our signature rich, lasting colours that grow more beautiful with time.

Hand Weaving
Our traditional looms produce the intricate patterns unique to Swat craftsmanship, each shawl taking days to complete.
✂️

Heritage

Weaving Stories into Fabric

01
Raw Material Selection
Every shawl begins from the finest Raah Maal — raw wool sourced from mountain farms in Swat, hand-picked for softness and quality.
02
Washing & Carding
Raw wool is washed in spring water and carded by hand to remove impurities while preserving natural oils and fibre integrity.
03
Precision Weaving
Master weavers work on hand looms, meticulously building the complex diamond and herringbone patterns thread by thread.
04
Hand-Fringe Finishing
The signature twisted fringe is finished entirely by hand, securing every warp thread into a durable border.
